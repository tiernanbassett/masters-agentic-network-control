
# Potential Research questions

-Can an Agentic controller be more flexible than current approaches? What are the pros and cons?
-What safety mechanisms can be included to make and agentic controller safe?
-Can an agentic controller truly safely control a network when safety mechanisms are in place?
-How does the tradeoff between latency, reasoning, and computational complexity affect an agentic controllers viability as an option for controlling a network?
